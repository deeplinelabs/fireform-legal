Account and data

Open Settings → Export or delete data for export and account deletion. Use Account for connected login methods. Cloud account actions require an internet connection.

Purchases

Sign in to the intended FireForm account, then use Settings → Restore purchases with the Apple account that purchased the subscription. Manage or cancel in Apple subscription settings. Account deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Camera and reminders

Use Privacy and permissions to open iPhone Settings. Camera permission is optional; manual workout logging stays available. Reminders can be changed or disabled in Workout reminders.
