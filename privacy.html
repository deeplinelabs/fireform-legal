PRIVACY POLICY

Your workout. Your control.

Last updated: 4 October 2026 · Current development build

This notice describes the current FireForm iPhone app by DeepLine Labs. It does not describe features that have not shipped. Public release requires a final review of the developer contact details and operational retention practices.

Camera and movement analysis

When you choose a camera workout and grant permission, FireForm processes camera frames and pose landmarks on your iPhone to count repetitions and show available form cues. Camera video and pose streams are not saved or uploaded by the app. Manual workouts remain available without camera permission.

Your account and completed workouts

Supabase provides sign-in, account storage and completed-workout sync. Account information can include your account ID, email, chosen display name and connected sign-in methods. Completed workouts include recorded exercise, repetitions, sets, timing and related workout metadata. Your account ID associates records with you. Local drafts and unsynced workouts stay on the device until saved, synced, discarded or deleted.

Apple and Google handle their respective sign-in services when you use them. FireForm uses the resulting account authentication to sign you in; do not send authentication tokens or passwords to support.

Local plans and preferences

Personal Trainer plans, progression history, recovery check-ins, optional effort, reminder preferences and appearance preferences are stored on the device. The current app does not synchronize raw camera data or recovery check-ins to your account. Data export describes which records are device-only.

Subscriptions

Apple processes App Store purchases. RevenueCat checks the fireform_pro entitlement using your FireForm account ID and purchase/subscription information. The integration does not send your email, display name, camera video, workout records or raw Health samples to RevenueCat. A restored Apple purchase is checked against the signed-in FireForm account.

Optional Apple Watch and Health

Apple Watch and Health integration is currently disabled in the normal app configuration. If enabled in a future or designated build, it requires explicit permission and uses heart rate and active energy during workouts you start. Raw Health samples are not included in account sync or analytics. That release will receive an updated notice.

Notifications, advertising and analytics

Workout reminders are optional local notifications scheduled on your device. The current build does not include an advertising SDK or a third-party product-analytics integration. System and service providers may process technical connection, security, crash or purchase diagnostics under their own policies.

Retention, export and deletion

Saved workout and account records remain until you use the relevant deletion controls. FireForm-data deletion removes this app’s data while retaining the shared account. Entire-account deletion removes the shared account and its associated app data after the server completes the request. A pending or failed deletion is not shown as completed. Provider operational logs, purchase records and backups may follow separate retention requirements; operational retention details must be confirmed before public release.

In Settings, open Export or delete data to prepare an export, delete FireForm data or delete the entire account. Uninstalling the app removes its device storage but does not delete your cloud account or cancel an App Store subscription. Exported files you share or save outside the app remain under your control.

Your choices and requests

You can change camera and notification permissions in iPhone Settings, turn reminders off, use manual workouts, export your data and use the in-app deletion controls. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing and request portability, and may contact your local data-protection authority. For a private request, contact: deeplinelabs@gmail.com

Service providers and this website

Service-provider processing can occur in the regions used by those services. Refer to Supabase’s privacy notice, RevenueCat’s privacy notice and Apple’s privacy notice. This temporary website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no analytics, forms, cookies set by its own code or external fonts. GitHub may process ordinary connection information when you load it: GitHub privacy statement.

Changes

We will update this notice when the app’s data flows change. Any future advertising, remote AI, avatar upload or health-data sharing requires an updated notice before it is introduced.
