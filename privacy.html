PRIVACY POLICY

Your workout. Your control.

Last updated: 12 October 2026 · FireForm V1

FireForm is operated by Balog Alex, based in Hungary, under the DeepLine Labs brand. Contact: deeplinelabs@gmail.com. This notice describes FireForm V1 and the data it uses to provide your account, workouts and subscriptions.

V1 workout tracking

V1 uses manual repetition and set logging. It does not request camera access or collect video or pose streams. Historical workout summaries from earlier development builds can remain on the device until deleted.

Your account and completed workouts

Supabase provides sign-in, account storage and completed-workout sync. Account information can include your account ID, email, chosen display name and connected sign-in methods. Completed workouts include recorded exercise, repetitions, sets, timing and related workout metadata. Your account ID associates records with you. Local drafts and unsynced workouts stay on the device until saved, synced, discarded or deleted.

Apple and Google handle their respective sign-in services when you use them. FireForm uses the resulting account authentication to sign you in; do not send authentication tokens or passwords to support.

Local plans and preferences

Personal Trainer plans, progression history, recovery check-ins, optional effort, reminder preferences and appearance preferences are stored on the device. The current app does not synchronize raw camera data or recovery check-ins to your account. Data export describes which records are device-only.

Subscriptions

Apple processes App Store purchases. RevenueCat checks the fireform_pro entitlement using your FireForm account ID and purchase/subscription information. The integration does not send your email, display name, camera video, workout records or raw Health samples to RevenueCat. A restored Apple purchase is checked against the signed-in FireForm account. The RevenueCat paywall also records its own views, closes and purchase-flow interactions linked to that account ID to operate and measure the subscription flow. FireForm adds no separate workout or product-funnel analytics integration.

Apple Watch and Health

V1 has no Apple Watch integration and does not request Apple Health access. Historical local Watch summaries from earlier development builds may remain on the device; raw Health samples are not included in FireForm account sync.

Notifications, advertising and analytics

Workout reminders are optional local notifications scheduled on your device. The current build does not include an advertising SDK or a third-party product-analytics integration. System and service providers may process technical connection, security, crash or purchase diagnostics under their own policies.

Retention, export and deletion

Saved workout and account records remain until you use the relevant deletion controls. FireForm-data deletion removes this app’s data while retaining the shared account. Entire-account deletion removes the shared account and its associated app data after the server completes the request. A pending or failed deletion is not shown as completed. Deletion-request receipts expire after seven days. Completed receipts are removed by the daily cleanup job after expiry; incomplete requests may remain until reconciled. Temporary in-app export files are removed at the next app launch or after 24 hours when cleanup runs; files you save elsewhere remain under your control. We do not create separate scheduled database backups for V1. Provider infrastructure copies, security logs and legally required purchase records follow the provider’s applicable retention and deletion practices; deleting a FireForm account does not erase Apple’s purchase records or cancel billing. Supabase Free currently makes approximately one day of service logs available to us; that access window is not a promise that every provider infrastructure copy is physically erased at that time. We do not keep an additional copy of these logs. Support correspondence is retained while resolving a request and for up to 90 days after closure, except where a legal obligation requires longer retention.

In Settings, open Export or delete data to prepare an export, delete FireForm data or delete the entire account. Uninstalling the app removes its device storage but does not delete your cloud account or cancel an App Store subscription. Exported files you share or save outside the app remain under your control.

Your choices and requests

You can change notification permissions in iPhone Settings, turn reminders off, use manual workouts, export your data and use the in-app deletion controls. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing and request portability, and may contact your local data-protection authority. For a private request, contact: deeplinelabs@gmail.com

Service providers and this website

The FireForm Supabase project is hosted in the EU West (Ireland) region. Apple, Google, RevenueCat and GitHub may process data internationally under their applicable terms and safeguards. Refer to Supabase’s privacy notice, RevenueCat’s privacy notice and Apple’s privacy notice. This temporary website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no analytics, forms, cookies set by its own code or external fonts. GitHub may process ordinary connection information when you load it: GitHub privacy statement.

Why we process data

Account, workout and purchase processing is needed to provide the service you request. Limited security and support processing helps us protect and operate it. Optional reminders remain on your device and can be disabled. We do not sell personal data or use it for cross-app advertising tracking.

Changes

We will update this notice when the app’s data flows change. Any future advertising, remote AI, avatar upload or health-data sharing requires an updated notice before it is introduced.
