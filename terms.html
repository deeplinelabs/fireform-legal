Last updated: 24 October 2026 · FireForm V1

This page explains the current FireForm experience. The Apple Standard End User License Agreement provides the App Store application license unless a different agreement is supplied in App Store Connect. This overview does not replace your statutory rights or Apple’s applicable terms.

Free and Personal Trainer features

Manual workouts, technique guides, local history and workout sync are free. FireForm Pro unlocks Personal Trainer programs, prescribed sets/reps/rest and progression review. Optional AI coach conversations and explanations may be available with separate permission and service configuration. New generations have per-account and shared availability limits, can be unavailable, and are not an unlimited or guaranteed chat service. AI text may be inaccurate and does not change your prescribed workout. V1 records repetitions manually. V1 does not require Apple Watch, camera or Apple Health permissions.

App Store subscriptions

The paywall displays the available monthly and annual plans, the full billing amount, currency and billing period supplied by the App Store. Prices are not fixed on this page. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled under Apple’s applicable terms. Any eligible introductory offer and subsequent billing amount must be shown in the purchase flow. No trial is promised by this page.

Manage or cancel through Apple subscription settings. Restore purchases is available explicitly in FireForm Settings and on the purchase paywall. Deleting FireForm data or your account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Apple handles refund requests under its applicable process.

Checking or purchasing a subscription requires an internet connection. If subscription access cannot be verified, Trainer may be unavailable; manual Free Workout remains available. Cancellation normally preserves access until the paid period ends. Refunds and expiry are applied when Apple and RevenueCat update the subscription status.

Exercise guidance

FireForm provides workout logging and general fitness guidance. Exercise guidance can be imperfect, and the app does not provide medical diagnosis, injury prediction or guaranteed results. Choose exercises and loads appropriate to your ability, follow the exercise prerequisites, and stop if you experience pain or feel unwell. Seek qualified advice when you need personalized medical or exercise assessment.

Account and device data

Use the account you intend to associate with your workout records and subscription. Some plans, check-ins and preferences are device-local; reinstalling or changing devices can affect that local data. See the privacy policy and the in-app export summary.
